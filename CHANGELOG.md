# Changelog

What changes for teachers in each release of Minhajna, newest first. How these notes are written is in [releases.md](docs/contributing/releases.md#the-changelog).

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-20)

The documents preview: the texts book, the daily journal, the roll-call book and the other paper books, printed from what you record, with the app lock, tests and exam weeks. It is still for trying the app with made-up pupils only, and is handed over directly as an APK, not through a store.

### New

- You can log a session outside the timetable (support, catch-up, review or supervised study) from the plus on Today or from a class's plan. You choose the class, the kind and the day, and type the times as in the paper book; the app never records when you tapped. A topic is optional and stays a private note on your own devices. These sessions do not move the plan, and they go on the texts book's page for additional lessons.
- You can add a make-up, support or revision day the school sets, on the timetable of a weekday you choose, even on a Saturday or in the holidays. You pick its sessions, and see what changes before adding it. Make-up sessions are plan sessions and give your classes more room before the exams; support and revision sessions do not move the plan. Today marks the day.
- The texts book keeps its homework record. Each homework you write in a session's details shows under «Homework», from the texts book, with the day it was set and the day it comes back. When it comes back, the class's card on Today says so, and you enter two counts: pupils who did not do it, and pupils who relied on someone else. Each shows as a share of the class list, and no name is ever kept.
- Each class card in the Classes tab now shows «القسم» beside «دفتر النصوص», in place of the plan, which opens from the Class page: the class's subject and pupils, chips to switch to the teacher's other classes of the subject, today's session with its roll call, and a row for each of its records with what it holds, such as the texts book's last session and page, the month's absences, lateness and attendance so far, in half-days, or the homework due today. Each pupil shows the month's absences and lateness so far. The settings button at the top opens the seating plan, the counting rules and pupil moves. Assessment comes in a later version.
- Each class card on the Classes tab opens its texts book: the pages as they print, page by page, in the subject's language. Sessions awaiting confirmation show in grey with what the book would take, and one tap confirms a proposed lesson, with Undo. A confirmed row opens its details to correct it, and any month prints from the page on screen.
- The Class page's roll-call row opens the roll-call book. A month shows its Gregorian and Hijri months, the paper book's legend and a table of the class's session days up to today, opening on the latest: each half-day missed is marked «-» for a morning, «ǀ» for an afternoon and «+» for a whole day, in brackets when justified, and «+م½» for a whole day with one half justified; each pupil's absences, attendance and lateness follow, and the class's totals and rate under the table. A tap on a day opens its roll call, for example to enter a paper sheet; a day with several roll calls lists them first. The yearly summary gives each month and the year for every pupil, with the class's totals, up to June unless a roll call was taken in July.
- A pupil's page in the roll-call book opens from the Class page and from their name in the month and yearly tables. For a month so far or the school year it gives their possible and actual attendance, absences and rate in half-days, each half-day missed with a «مبرر / غير مبرر» toggle that records the change at once, their late arrivals, and a change log of when each mark was recorded and each change with its reason, newest first. «تصحيح غياب أو تأخر مسجّل» lists the roll calls to correct, and the year links the class's yearly summary.
- «مواعيد الفروض», from the class plan and the class page, holds each class's tests. A test takes one of the class's sessions, picked on a month grid, and becomes a fixed row the lessons flow around. Before it is saved, the screen shows the sessions it takes, the item that moves, and any item it would push past the exams. The next exams show from the calendar, with how sure their date is. A test can be removed, and «تراجع» takes back an addition or a removal.
- When a class's plan would push items past its next exams, a warning card shows on Today and at the top of the class plan until the teacher decides. «ما العمل؟» lists the items with the day each would fall on. The teacher can merge items or leave out optional ones, with a suggestion ticked and the result shown before confirming; add a make-up session; or postpone the items to the first sessions after the exams, where the plan tags them until they are taught. «لاحقاً» keeps the warning, nothing applies before a choice, and «تراجع» takes a choice back. A session with two merged items proposes both.
- During an exam week, «إضافة حراسة أو اختبار» on Today and under the exams row of «مواعيد الفروض» lets the teacher type an invigilation or a class exam from the school's paper: the day, chosen among exam days only, the times, the room and, for a class exam, the classes. Today shows the day's entries, each can be removed, and «تراجع» takes a save or a removal back. Nothing is counted, and an invigilation has its own row on its day in the journal for all classes.
- The new Documents tab lists every document below, for the levels you teach. Each one prints through your phone's print service, or saves as a PDF wherever you choose. You can print it as recorded, or blank with its headings to fill in by hand. Documents with pupils' names carry «داخلي · سري» at the head and foot of every page.
- The Documents tab lists the texts book: this month's pages for each class and subject you teach, laid out like the paper book, with the date, the time, what was done and the homework of every session you confirmed. The heads are in Arabic, and a French or English subject's pages are in its language, left to right. Pages are numbered from the start of the year, and the signature column stays blank to sign by hand. It also prints blank, with its heads filled in.
- «تصحيحات دفتر النصوص», on the Documents tab and in the texts book, shows a month's corrections for the director's check: each corrected session or extra session with what it was and what it is, the day of the change and its reason, and a note when the page was printed before. It prints or saves as a PDF, and a month without corrections says so.
- The Documents tab lists the texts book's page 08, supervised or additional lessons: for each class, every session you logged outside the timetable and every session of a support or revision day, numbered through the year, with its subject, kind, date and time. A class with none gets the page blank, with its headings.
- The homework record prints: «Homework record» on the Documents tab gives each class and subject its texts book page for the school year, with each homework's dates, its activity and the two counts with their percentages, left blank until counted. It also prints blank, for filling in by hand.
- For schools that accept it, «Strip to paste» prints a day's confirmed entries at the paper texts book's column widths and line spacing, inside crop marks, a piece for each class and subject. Each piece says the page and the line it goes after. It opens from the texts book for the last day of the page on screen, or from the Documents tab for today.
- The daily journal prints each session as the CEM and lycée journal asks: its unit, its field when it is not a lesson, the resource, «continued» with the stages still to cover, how the session went and the remarks, with the times as the book writes them by hand. A session awaiting confirmation shows what confirming it would record. The journal's output chooses the day, and whether the teacher keeps one journal for all classes or one per class.
- The daily journal's front pages print from the Documents tab: the teacher's card with the director's visa, the seminars, training and meetings in the calendar, those still to come in grey, and the year's holidays and national days, each announced, expected or projected. Empty rules follow for what is written by hand. They also print blank.
- A primary teacher's daily journal prints: «Primary daily journal» on the Documents tab gives each primary class an A4 landscape page for the day, with the morning's and the afternoon's sessions, each with its duration, subject, activity, content and domain, and boxes for the teacher's signature and the director's visa. The competence indicator and the lesson note's number are left to write by hand. The Documents tab lists each journal only for the levels the teacher teaches.
- The primary journal's front pages print: «Primary daily journal · Front pages» on the Documents tab gives each primary class a cover, the teacher's card with the class's number of pupils, holidays and national days, seminars and training, the pupil list with the number, the name and the registration number only, and the class's weekly timetable with boxes for the director's approval, each page marked «Internal · confidential».
- Primary French, English and Tamazight specialists get their own daily journal: «Specialist's daily journal» on the Documents tab prints the day's sessions on an A4 landscape page in the subject's language, with a band for each school and half-day and a director's visa for each school, or a page for each class. The class teacher's journal and its front pages leave those subjects out.
- The weekly timetable prints: «Weekly timetable» on the Documents tab gives the teacher's week on an A4 portrait page, with each slot's bell time, each class's hours and the total, the day the timetable applies from, and boxes for the teacher's signature and the director's and inspector's visas.
- «طباعة دفتر المناداة» opens from the Month and yearly tables, the Class page and the Documents tab. It prints a class's month as the paper book's two A4 portrait pages, days 1 to 20 with the names, then the rest with «غ», «ح-ف», each pupil's absence dates and late arrivals, the totals so far and the signature boxes of the teacher, the director and the inspector; weekends and holidays are grey, days with none of your sessions with the class are dotted, and the Hijri months are named. The rows run to 50, empty ones numbered for a newcomer. It also prints the yearly summary on one A4 landscape page. Both print as recorded or blank, or save as a PDF, and the boxes stay blank to sign by hand.
- «طباعة دفتر المناداة» also prints the book's front pages: the cover with the class, the year, the teacher and the boys, girls and total; the class list with registration numbers; and the two confidential pupil-data pages to fill by hand. It also prints «ورقة مناداة فارغة», one A4 page for one of the class's session days, with a box per roll call for each pupil, to take the roll on paper and enter it later.
- In CEM and lycée, «طباعة دفتر المناداة» prints «ورقة غياب الحصة» for a taken roll call: the class, day, session and teacher, then only the absent and late pupils, or «لا غياب ولا تأخر», with a box for the education counsellor, on the top half of an A4 page to cut. Once a roll call is taken, the print icon on its screen opens its slip.
- Until a roll call is taken, the print icon on its screen opens the blank roll-call sheet of its class and day.
- «طباعة دفتر المناداة» prints «مخطط الجلوس»: each of the class's seating plans on its own A4 page, as arranged, with the board on top, full names on the seats and empty boxes to write in. The seating setup screen has a print icon for its plan.
- «Print the week» opens from the top of the Documents tab: the documents with something new since their last print are ticked, each with its range, such as a month or two dates, and its pages. It prints on one or two sides with binding margins and a blank copy if asked, gives the pages, sheets and cost, and prints or saves one PDF for the print shop. The choices stay on the phone, and each print is kept in the teacher's history.
- In pilot builds, the phone times how long confirming each session and taking each roll call takes, in seconds only, with no day or time. «توقيتاتي في التجربة», at the end of the Documents tab, shows the median, how many sessions took one tap and the minutes a week. Nothing is sent: the teacher can share the figures through the phone's share sheet, or clear them.

### Changed

- In middle and secondary school, the roll-call totals now count half-days, as the paper roll-call book does: missing one or more of your sessions in a morning or an afternoon counts 1, and a whole day 2. The roll is still taken each session.
- The school year now reads «2026-2027» after Arabic words, in the plan's national calendar and in every document, instead of «2027-2026».

### Fixed

- Correcting one pupil in a roll call no longer fails when another pupil marked in it has since changed group; only the pupil corrected must still be on the roll call's list.
- If you correct your week during setup on a later day, before the school year begins, the correction now stays once the year starts, instead of the week you drew first coming back.

### Privacy

- This pilot build times how long confirming each session and taking each roll call take: durations in seconds, kept on this phone with only the school week they fall in, never a day, a time of day or what was taught. Nothing is sent. The figures leave the phone only if you share them yourself from «توقيتاتي في التجربة», where you can also clear them.
- Documents are made on this phone, without the internet. A PDF goes only where you save it; the copy the app makes while preparing it is deleted once you save or cancel, and at every start. Printing goes through the print service you choose on your phone, which may pass the pages to your printer's own service.

### Security

- The app now locks. You choose a 4-digit PIN at first use, and can open the app with your phone's fingerprint or face too; the app keeps neither. It locks after 5 minutes unused, and each wrong PIN after the fourth makes the wait longer, up to an hour. A history from 0.1.0 keeps its records under the new PIN. In «Lock and security», from Help, you can change the PIN, turn the fingerprint or face on or off, and choose 1, 5 or 15 minutes. Screens with pupils' records are hidden from screenshots and recent apps unless you allow screenshots there.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-18)

The field-check preview: setup, Today and the roll call, for trying the app with made-up pupils only. It is handed over directly as an APK, not through a store.

### New

- Setup now starts after the privacy notice: pick your levels and subjects, two subjects included, and name your school. A teacher in more than one school adds each one, with its own levels and subjects. Every choice is saved on this phone as you make it.
- Setup now asks for each school's wilaya, from the list of 69 in Law 26-06. Find it by name or number. It picks your wilaya's dates in the calendar and never leaves this phone.
- Setup's third step adds your classes and lets you type each class's list: name, sex and, if you have it, the registration number. Each pupil is saved as you add them, the list is sorted alphabetically, and a class can be any size. This trial version asks for made-up names only.
- Setup can add a demo class of 45 pupils with made-up names, for trying the app, taking screenshots or reporting a problem without real pupils' names.
- A primary teacher can add a joint class: two or three years in one room, each on its own plan. Each pupil's year is chosen as the list is typed, each year gets its own pack at the plan step, Today shows each slot as one card with every year's proposal, confirmed in one tap, and the Classes tab gives each year its row.
- Setup's fourth step draws your week by hand: pick a class, or your own time, and tap its slots. Saturday and a fifth morning slot can be added, each class's sessions are counted, and a changed week starts on the day you draw it, without changing any recorded session.
- A slot of your week can now hold a half-group, alternate between A and B weeks, name its subject and room, or hold your own time, such as a pedagogical half-day or hours in another school. Each subject of a class keeps its own plan and book, and clashes are named.
- Your week can now follow a double shift, mornings one week and afternoons the next. If you teach in two schools, each class shows its school, sessions are counted by school, and clashes are outlined and listed, with a button that opens the slot to settle them.
- Each slot of your week now has its time at the school, from the level's usual hours. You can set any slot to match your school's bell. In Ramadan, taken from the calendar, sessions keep their places and start times and are shortened, and your timetable stays as it is.
- Setup now ends with your plan: for each grade and subject, the pack your classes are pinned to, its status, release and source, or typed items when there is no pack. Each class stays on its release until you choose another. The national calendar is built in.
- Setup now ends on a summary of what it kept and the September check. For each class, it shows the sessions your timetable and the calendar give before the term's exams, those the plan needs, and guided work counted apart. The gap is shown as spare sessions for remediation, or as sessions short of the plan. Then comes your next session, with what the plan proposes.
- Today lists the day's sessions in order. The next one shows what the plan proposes, in pencil, and one tap on «Done as proposed» records it. Nothing is recorded before that tap, and «Undo» brings back what was there.
- «Another outcome» on Today records how a session really went, in two taps: the last stage reached, an item still going on or finished, merged with the next one, a skipped lesson, a lesson taught again, or a session not held. A reason for a skip or a session not held is optional and stays on the teacher's devices.
- «Another outcome» names the session where what is left goes on, by its day, and says how many sessions an item without stages has taken. A session not held can be marked to be made up.
- «Session details» on Today records a session in full, in one step: its type, the stages covered, content from outside the plan, homework with its due day, a test, a line for the texts book, and a private note that never leaves the teacher's devices. The row the texts book will take is shown as it changes.
- «Change the lesson» in a session's details writes another item of the plan instead of the proposed one, teaches an earlier item again, or writes content from outside the plan. The plan around the class shows the day each item was taught or would be reached, and the proposed item stays first for next time.
- «Correct» on a recorded session opens its details as they were recorded. A correction keeps the first version, can take a detail out, carries an optional coded reason, and lists what the activity log will add, by day. The words of a reason of the teacher's own stay on their devices.
- Today lists the earlier sessions still awaiting confirmation, a week at a time, and confirms them as proposed in one tap. A tap leaves a session out, so its other outcome or its details can be recorded with the day it was taught.
- Today marks holidays, closures and exam weeks with their name. The sessions a holiday takes are counted apart, never as slow teaching; in an exam week the plan pauses; and the next session is shown.
- «Roll call» on a session's card on Today opens its roll call: everyone is present, a tap marks an absence or a lateness, and one save records them; a past session is entered from the paper sheet on its own date, and a roll call already taken is corrected with a reason.
- «Enter a roll call from the paper sheet» in Today's earlier sessions lists the past sessions whose roll call is not recorded; each opens its roll call, recorded on the session's own date.
- On a roll call already taken, a pupil's name opens its correction: the mark it becomes, present, late or an absence justified or not, with a coded reason and the month's totals before and after. «Counting rules» sets the day counting starts and whether training days and sessions not held count.
- «Add or move» records a pupil who joins the class, at the end of the list from their day, or who leaves it, with the day and a coded reason. A pupil who leaves stays on the roll call with a note until the director's confirmation is recorded, and numbers stay as on the paper book.
- «Arrange the seats» draws a class's or group's room once, with its rows, seats and aisles, and places each pupil on a seat. Pupils can be placed in list order, moved or swapped. Each change is saved at once and stays on the teacher's devices.
- The roll call can be taken on the seating plan. A tap on a seat marks an absence, a second a lateness, a third the pupil present again, the same marks as the list.
- The Classes tab shows each class with its pupils, its first session today and where it stands against the plan, in sessions: the buffers used first, then any delay beyond them, or the spare sessions before the next exams.
- «Plan» on a class opens its plan item by item: the days each item was taught or would be reached, its stages, where the class is, and where the plan places it, as a reference only, with the sessions left before the exams.
- The app says it is a preview, for trying it with made-up pupils only. Help shows its version and the checksums of the app and of its signing certificate, so you can check that it is the project's preview.
- Help now shows the app's licence and all bundled notices offline. Opening the public source in your browser requires your confirmation.
- Android downloads come as a universal package that installs on any phone, and a smaller package for each phone processor type.

### Privacy

- Before first use, a new welcome shows the app's promises, and a privacy notice says what the app keeps and where: everything stays on this phone, and this version never connects to the internet. The notice, and what the app connects to, stay available in Help.
- Setup's second step is the teacher card, the details the documents print. Your name is optional and personal: it stays on this phone, out of every sync, export and share, and only the documents you make here print it.
- Reading licence notices stays offline. Only after a separate confirmation can the app hand the public source address to your browser, which may connect to GitHub; no record or identifier is attached.
