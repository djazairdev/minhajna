- en: The app now locks. You choose a 4-digit PIN at first use, and can open the app with your phone's fingerprint or face too; the app keeps neither. It locks after 5 minutes unused, and each wrong PIN after the fourth makes the wait longer, up to an hour. A history from 0.1.0 keeps its records under the new PIN.
