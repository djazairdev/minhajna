- en: In CEM and lycée, «طباعة دفتر المناداة» prints «ورقة غياب الحصة» for a taken roll call: the class, day, session and teacher, then only the absent and late pupils, or «لا غياب ولا تأخر», with a box for the education counsellor, on the top half of an A4 page to cut.
