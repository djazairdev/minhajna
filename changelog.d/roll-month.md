- en: The Class page's roll-call row opens the roll-call book. A month shows its Gregorian and Hijri months, the paper book's legend and a table of the class's session days up to today, opening on the latest: each half-day missed is marked «-» for a morning, «ǀ» for an afternoon and «+» for a whole day, in brackets when justified, and «+م½» for a whole day with one half justified; each pupil's absences, attendance and lateness follow, and the class's totals and rate under the table. A tap on a day opens its roll call, for example to enter a paper sheet; a day with several roll calls lists them first. The yearly summary gives each month and the year for every pupil, with the class's totals, up to June unless a roll call was taken in July.
