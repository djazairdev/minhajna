- en: «Print the week» opens from the top of the Documents tab: the documents with something new since their last print are ticked, each with its range, such as a month or two dates, and its pages. It prints on one or two sides with binding margins and a blank copy if asked, gives the pages, sheets and cost, and prints or saves one PDF for the print shop. The choices stay on the phone, and each print is kept in the teacher's history.
