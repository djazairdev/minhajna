# Code of conduct

[العربية](#كيف-نشارك-باحترام) · [English](#code-of-conduct)

Minhajna brings together teachers, directors, inspectors, developers and everyone else who wants to help Algeria's schools. This code sets how we treat each other in the project's spaces. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant ([below](#attribution)), with rules of our own on pupils' data and on keeping the project's spaces about the product ([PRD §1.11](docs/prd/PRD.md#111-community-transparency-and-building-in-public)).

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome and safe in the project's spaces, whatever their age, sex, disability, origin, language, religion, beliefs or experience, and whatever their role: teacher, director, inspector, parent or developer. We act and speak in ways that build an open, respectful and healthy community.

## What we expect

- Respect for other people and their experience, even in disagreement.
- Criticism of the work, never of the person, given and taken with care.
- Owning our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Thinking of the whole community, and of the teachers and pupils the project serves.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, and personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a phone number or an address, without their explicit permission.
- Political or union campaigning, and attacks on named people, whether officials, colleagues, pupils or parents.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find out of place in a professional setting.

## Rules of our own

- **Never post real pupil data:** names, marks, absences or photos, anywhere in the project. Use made-up names or the demo class ([PRD §1.5](docs/prd/PRD.md#15-pupil-data-and-privacy-rules)).
  - Civil-service secrecy covers pupils' marks and attendance (Ord. 06-03 Art. 48), and publishing through a platform run from abroad, such as GitHub or Facebook, counts as a transfer abroad (ANPDP deliberation 04).
  - Moderators remove such a post as soon as they see it, and tell its author privately why. A removal protects pupils; it is not a sanction.
- **No images of pupils or staff** in any project channel.
- **The project takes no side** in disputes between teachers and the administration. Discussions stay about the product, and cite the official texts.

## Where it applies

In all the project's spaces: the GitHub organisation, the website and its form, the teachers' Facebook group, support chats and events. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Who looks after it

The community moderators, the maintainers and the lead maintainer ([GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)). They may remove, edit or reject comments, posts, issues, pull requests and other contributions that break this code, and they explain their decision when it helps.

## Reporting a problem

- **On GitHub,** use "Report content" on the comment, issue or pull request, and send the report to the repository's maintainers. Only they see it.
- **In the teachers' Facebook group,** report the post or comment to the group's admins.
- **Never report in public,** and never put real pupil data in a report.
- **Security problems** go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Every report is reviewed and answered promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person who reported it is respected. The project will add an address of its own for reports when it has one.

## What can happen

Those who look after this code use these steps, from the lightest to the most serious.

1. **Correction.** For words or behaviour that were out of place. A private note explaining what was wrong, and sometimes a public apology.
2. **Warning.** For a serious breach, or a series of breaches. A warning with consequences: no contact with the people involved for a set time, including with those enforcing the code. A further breach can lead to a ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious breach, including continued behaviour after a warning. No interaction with the community for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of breaches, harassment of a person, or hostility towards a group of people. No interaction with the community, for good.

Posting real pupil data by mistake leads to its removal and a private explanation. Posting it again, or on purpose, is a serious breach.

## كيف نشارك باحترام؟

هذه خلاصة [قواعد السلوك الكاملة بالإنجليزية](#code-of-conduct).

- ناقش العمل، لا صاحبه. لا تحرّش أو إهانة أو تهديد أو تجريح شخصي.
- النقاش عن التطبيق، لا للدعاية السياسية أو النقابية أو الهجوم على أشخاص بأسمائهم.
- لا تنشر بيانات تلاميذ حقيقيين أو صور تلاميذ أو موظفين، ولا معلومات شخصية عن أي شخص دون إذنه. استعمل القسم التجريبي في صور الشاشة.
- إن نشرت بيانات تلاميذ بالخطأ، أبلغ المشرف بشكل خاص ليزيلها ويحميهم.

### كيف أطلب المساعدة؟

للملاحظات والاقتراحات: [صفحة التحميل والتجربة](https://djazair.dev/ar/minhajna/#feedback).

للإبلاغ عن إساءة، تواصل مع المشرفين بشكل خاص:

- في مجموعة فيسبوك: أبلغ مشرفي المجموعة.
- على GitHub: استعمل «Report content» لإبلاغ مشرفي المشروع.

لا تنشر الاتهامات علنًا.

لمشكلات الأمن والخصوصية: [الإبلاغ الخاص](SECURITY.md). لا تُرفق بيانات تلاميذ، حتى في رسالة خاصة.
